10. Tuition Agreement
  • Tuition is due on the first business day of each month.
  • A late fee of $25.00 will be applied after the 5th of the month.
  • The annual registration fee is non-refundable.
  • Two weeks' written notice is required for withdrawal; tuition is due during the notice period.
  • Tuition is not prorated for absences, holidays, or academy closures.
  • Payment plans are available; contact the director for arrangements.
  • Returned check fee is $35.00.
  • Drop-in care is subject to availability and charged at a daily rate.
  • Sibling discounts are available; contact the director for details.
  • The academy closes for major holidays; see the academic calendar for specific dates.
  • Late pick-up (after closing time) incurs a fee of $1.00 per minute.
  • Only individuals listed on the authorized pick-up list may collect the child; photo ID required.
  • Children must be signed in and out each day.
  • The academy is not responsible for lost or damaged personal items.
  • Children must be up-to-date on immunizations; Form 3231 required.
  • Sick children must be kept home; a doctor's note may be required for return.
  • Medication will only be administered with a completed medication authorization form.
  • Field trip permission is included in this agreement unless otherwise noted in writing.
  • The academy reserves the right to terminate enrollment for non-payment or policy violations.
  • All fees and policies are subject to change with 30 days' written notice.
  • By signing below, I acknowledge that I have read and agree to all terms of this tuition agreement.
