Welcome to KIPLA

A positive start shapes everything that follows

KIPLA is a family-owned early learning community in Austell, Georgia, with two campuses serving scholars from 6 weeks through 12 years. From the first morning, your child is known by name, story, and strength.

The KIPLA difference: we pair leadership-centered learning designed to prepare scholars for every next step with genuine warmth — every scholar is taught, trained, motivated, and loved every single day.

Meet Our Founder

Meet Ms. Wanda

Founder & Visionary • Educator • Mentor • Community Leader

For more than 20 years, Ms. Wanda has dedicated her life to educating children, strengthening families, and developing future leaders. What began as a passion for helping children learn grew from tutoring students at home into what is now Keeping It Positive Learning Academy (KIPLA).

With a master’s degree in Human Services and decades of hands-on experience in education and leadership, Ms. Wanda built KIPLA on a philosophy that reaches beyond academics. She believes children should be taught with excellence, trained with intention, motivated with purpose, and loved through every stage of their development.

That belief became the heart of KIPLA:

TEACHTRAINMOTIVATELOVE

Today, her vision continues to grow through KIPLA’s programs, educators, families, and commitment to developing confident, capable young leaders.

For Ms. Wanda, KIPLA is more than a learning academy—it is the continuation of a vision planted years ago: to pour something positive into a child today that will remain with them for a lifetime.

Her mission remains simple:
Plant the seed. Nurture the potential. Grow the leader. 🌻

— Ms. Wanda
