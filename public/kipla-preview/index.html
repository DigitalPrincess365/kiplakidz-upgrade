Welcome to KIPLA

A positive start shapes everything that follows

KIPLA is a family-owned early learning community in Austell, Georgia, with two campuses serving scholars from 6 weeks through 12 years. From the first morning, your child is known by name, story, and strength.

The KIPLA difference: we pair leadership-centered learning designed to prepare scholars for every next step with genuine warmth — every scholar is taught, trained, motivated, and loved every single day.

The KIPLA Difference

Teach • Train • Motivate • Love

Four words guide everything we do — they are our promise to every scholar and every family.

Teach

Purposeful learning every day

Intentional, leadership-centered learning delivered by educators who plan for each scholar, not just each classroom.

Train

Leadership from the start

Scholars practice responsibility, problem-solving, and classroom leadership roles from the toddler years up.

Motivate

Confidence that carries

Morning affirmations, celebrated milestones, and positive language build children who believe in themselves.

Love

Family, not just care

Low teacher turnover, daily communication home, and a team that knows every child by name and story.

Culture in Action

Real classrooms, real learning

Photographs straight from our rooms — learning walls, centers, educators teaching, and scholars leading.

Meet Our Founder
“I built KIPLA to be the place I wanted for my own children — safe, joyful, and full of people who believe in them.”

With more than 20 years in education and a Master's degree in Human Services, our founder opened KIPLA to prove that early learning can be both academically strong and deeply loving. Her vision: two campuses where every scholar is taught with purpose, trained to lead, motivated daily, and loved like family.
