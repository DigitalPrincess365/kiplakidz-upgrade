Before & After School

5 – 12 years

Offered at KIPLA #1 + KIPLA #2

Enroll in this program Schedule a Tour

School-age scholars get a calm landing spot before the bell and a productive, joyful afternoon after it — homework first, then enrichment, games, and leadership activities with educators who know them by name.

What makes it special

  • Transportation to and from partner schools
  • Daily homework help and quiet study time
  • Snack, games, and enrichment clubs
  • Full-day care on school holidays

A day in this room

  • Early-morning drop-off, breakfast, and calm start
  • Transport to school
  • Afternoon pickup and snack
  • Homework help and reading time
  • Enrichment clubs, outdoor play, and free choice

What’s included

  • Morning and afternoon snack
  • School transportation (partner schools)
  • Holiday and teacher-workday coverage
