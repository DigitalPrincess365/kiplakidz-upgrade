Our Programs

Toddlers

1 – 2 years

Offered at KIPLA #1 + KIPLA #2

Enroll in this program Schedule a Tour

Toddlers are busy, curious, and learning words by the hour. Our toddler classroom channels all that energy into routines, songs, and hands-on centers that build vocabulary, independence, and confident little bodies.

What makes it special

  • Potty-training partnership with families
  • Daily music, movement, and outdoor play
  • Self-feeding and independence skills
  • Gentle guidance and social-emotional coaching

A day in this room

  • Morning greeting and free-choice centers
  • Circle time: songs, names, weather, colors
  • Art, sensory, and building exploration
  • Outdoor play and gross-motor time
  • Family-style meals, nap, and afternoon stories

What’s included

  • Cots and bedding for nap time
  • Potty-training supplies and support
  • Daily report with meals, naps, and moments

Seats are limited in every classroom. Start your enrollment or come see the room in person.
